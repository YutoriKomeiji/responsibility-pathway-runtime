# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-oriented, and accessible project environment for people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific about observations, environments, assumptions, and evidence.
- Separate verified behavior from inference, opinion, and requested future work.
- Critique ideas, code, documentation, and claims without attacking people.
- Protect private information, credentials, customer data, and security-sensitive details.
- Respect Human Gates, maintainer decisions, and the responsibility boundaries described by the project.
- Help make reports reproducible and understandable across languages and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of secrets or personal data, deliberate disruption, deceptive evidence, pressure to bypass safety or approval boundaries, and public disclosure of unpatched exploitable vulnerabilities are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; request clarification; restrict participation; or report conduct to the hosting platform when necessary. Security reports should use the private route in `SECURITY.md`.

Enforcement decisions remain human maintainer decisions. AI assistance may help organize evidence or draft responses but does not hold moderation authority or final responsibility.
